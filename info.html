Why should you join?

Freehold High School cross country is a team-focused sport that builds endurance, discipline, and teamwork throughout the season.

Runners regularly train to improve speed, stamina, and race strategy while supporting one another on and off the course.

Practice

  • Monday through Friday
  • After school training sessions
  • Focus on conditioning, pacing, and form

Meet Goals

  • Improve personal bests
  • Build team chemistry
  • Compete with effort and sportsmanship
